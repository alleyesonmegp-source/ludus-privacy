How to delete your Ludus account

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Option 1 — Directly from the app (immediate)

If you still have the app installed:

  1. Open Ludus and enter Parent Mode (PIN required).
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap "Delete account" (for the parent) or the remove icon next to a specific child in the switcher, if you only want to delete that child instead of the whole account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion is immediate.

Option 2 — If you no longer have access to the app

Write to propinosoftware@gmail.com stating the email address of the parent account to be deleted. We process every request manually within 30 days of receiving it.

Write to request deletion

What gets deleted

  • The parent account (authentication) and, if it is the only parent in the family, every linked child (account, devices, monitored apps, quiz/event history, uploaded icons).
  • If you request deletion of a single child (not the whole account), only that child's data is deleted: their device account, monitored apps, quiz/event history.

What remains and for how long

No personal data is retained after a completed deletion, except for any legal obligations that may apply in the future (none currently exist for this app). Email requests are deleted from our mailbox once the request has been fulfilled.
