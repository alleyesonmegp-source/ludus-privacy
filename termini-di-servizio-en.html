1. What Ludus is

Ludus is a parental control app that helps a parent or guardian manage the time a minor spends on certain apps on their Android phone, with optional features for reading an electronic school register and AI-based study support (Coach Ludus). It is designed to be set up and managed by an adult responsible for the minor, not by the minor themselves.

2. Free trial and subscription

  • Ludus includes a 30-day free trial with all features active, starting from the app's first launch (single-device use) or from the creation of the family (if you link a child).
  • After the trial ends, continuing to use the app requires a paid subscription with automatic renewal, at the price shown in the app at the time of purchase through Google Play Billing. Renewal happens automatically at the end of each period, unless cancelled.
  • Each parent pays for their own subscription individually, even when linked to the same family as other parents — not a single shared subscription. Children never pay: they have full access as long as at least one parent in the family has an active subscription, regardless of how many parents or child devices there are.

  • Coach Ludus credit packages are also available, purchasable separately from the subscription, and are non-refundable once consumed.

3. Acceptable use of Coach Ludus

Coach Ludus is restricted to responding only to school-related topics, with a filter that blocks requests for vulgar, inappropriate, or otherwise off-topic content (including any attempt to steer it away from its role as a school tutor). If a request is refused for this reason, Ludus does not generate any content and shows a message inviting the user to choose a school-related topic — the attempt still consumes a credit, just like a successful request, and is not refunded.
