What Ludus does, in short

Ludus is a parental control app for Android. It helps a parent limit the time a minor spends on certain apps on their phone, unlocking them through a short educational quiz instead of a plain block. A parent can manage their child's device remotely once the two devices are linked with an invite code.

Data we collect and why

  • Parent account data: if a parent links a child, sign-in happens with a real Google account (Google Sign-In: name, email, profile photo of the Google account used); for single-device use (no child linked) the app uses anonymous sign-in, without requesting any Google account data. Both are handled through Firebase Authentication.
  • Device identifier: a (non-reversible) hash of the phone's ANDROID_ID, used only to prevent abuse of the free trial period and Coach Ludus free credits (e.g. repeatedly resetting the app to keep getting new credits/free trial). It does not identify the person, only the physical device.
  • Family and child device data: the name chosen for the child, school level (elementary/middle/high school), the list of monitored apps and their settings (time limit, quiz subject/difficulty), device status (battery, connection), statistics on quizzes passed/failed.
  • Electronic school register (optional feature): if the parent enables it, Ludus reads the grades and homework status shown on screen by the school register app already installed on the child's device, to use as an additional unlock condition. This data stays on the device and is only synced between devices of the same family through Firebase — it is never sent to any artificial intelligence service.
  • Content generated with Coach Ludus (optional, AI-based feature): if the child uses the "Coach Ludus" feature to generate a mini-lesson, a practice quiz, or ask for help with a photo of homework or a book page, the subject, topic, and — only if the child chooses to take one — the photo are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the answer. The child's name, grades, or other identifying data are never sent along with these requests.
  • Subscription and purchase data: if the parent activates the Ludus subscription or buys a Coach Ludus credit package, the purchase happens through Google Play Billing. Ludus only receives and stores the subscription status (active/expired/trial), the expiry date, and a technical purchase identifier (used to verify it with Google and prevent duplicate purchases) — it never receives or stores payment card data, which is handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Child device location (only at the moment of an SOS alert, never in the background): if the child uses the SOS button to ask for help, the app tries to read the device's GPS location ONCE, at that exact moment, after requesting Android's location permission. If available, the location is included as a Google Maps link in the alert notification sent to the parent. Ludus never tracks location in the background or at any other time outside of an SOS alert triggered by the child themselves.

Who we share data with

  • Google Firebase (authentication, Firestore database, app icon storage, cloud functions, push notifications): the app's backend infrastructure.
  • Google Gemini: only for the Coach Ludus features described above, only when the child actively uses them.
  • Google Play Billing: for processing the subscription and credit packages, according to Google Play's privacy policies. Ludus verifies purchases through the Google Play Developer API, without ever directly handling payment data.

We do not sell or share data with advertisers. Ludus does not show ads.

The Accessibility permission (Accessibility Service)

Ludus requests Android's Accessibility permission for two purposes, both necessary for the app's core functionality:

  • Detecting which app is in the foreground on the child's device, to apply the time limits set by the parent and show the block overlay when time runs out.
  • Reading the grade/homework status from the electronic school register (only if the parent enables this optional feature), to use as an unlock condition.

Ludus does not use this permission to read the content of apps other than those explicitly monitored by the parent or the configured school register, nor to perform autonomous actions on the user's behalf.

Minors

Ludus is designed to be set up and managed by a parent or legal guardian, who knowingly chooses which data about their minor child is collected and which features (including AI-based ones) the child has access to.

Data retention and deletion

  • Activity events (quizzes passed/failed, blocks) are kept for a limited period and automatically deleted after that period.
  • A parent can remove a child from the family, or delete their own account, at any time directly from the app, from Settings (parent section) → "Delete account": this cascades to delete all associated data (account, devices, monitored apps, events, icons).
